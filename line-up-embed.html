Line Up
Put every card in its place.

Six rounds. Each one hands you a stack of cards and a rule for ordering them. Drag the cards from the tray onto the numbered line.

Press Check order when the line is full. You get four checks per round, and each one tells you how many cards sit in the right position. Hints place one card for you but cost points.
